Privacy Policy

Migraine Tracker  ·  Last updated: September 2026

🔒 Migraine Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Overview

Migraine Tracker is a personal health logging app that helps you track migraine days, pain levels, medications, and triggers. Your privacy is our highest priority — the app is designed to work entirely on your device with no external data collection of any kind.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Specifically, Migraine Tracker does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Health or medical data
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any information whatsoever

Data Storage

All data you enter — including migraine logs, pain levels, medications, and triggers — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is fully under your control at all times

Data Export

The app includes an optional CSV export feature. When you export your data, the file is saved directly to your device. We do not receive, store, or have access to any exported files.

Third-Party Services

Migraine Tracker does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data services. No third-party SDKs are included that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Migraine Tracker does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Since the app collects no data, any future changes would only reflect new features, not new data practices.
